Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new account?

To create a new account, click on the "Sign Up" button on the login page. Fill in your personal details, create a username and password, and submit the form. Your account will be reviewed and activated within 24 hours.

How do I reset my password?

Click on the "Forgot password?" link on the login page. Enter your registered email address, and we'll send you instructions to reset your password.

How do I update my personal information?

After logging in, navigate to your profile settings. You can update your contact information, address, and other personal details there.

How do I transfer funds to another account?

After logging in, go to the "Transfer" section. Enter the recipient's account number, the amount you wish to transfer, and your PIN to confirm the transaction.

What is the daily transfer limit?

The standard daily transfer limit is $10,000. For higher limits, please contact our customer service.

How long does it take for a transfer to be processed?

Transfers between RJM Bank accounts are processed instantly. Transfers to other banks typically take 1-2 business days.

How do I apply for a loan?

After logging in, navigate to the "Loans" section and select "Apply for a Loan." Fill in the required information, including the loan amount, purpose, and term. Your application will be reviewed, and you'll receive a decision within 2-3 business days.

What factors affect my loan approval?

Loan approval is based on several factors, including your CIBIL score, income, existing debts, and credit history.

How do I check my loan status?

You can check the status of your loan application in the "Loans" section of your account dashboard.

How does RJM Bank protect my account?

We use industry-standard encryption and multi-factor authentication to protect your account. All transactions require PIN verification.

What should I do if I suspect unauthorized activity?

If you notice any suspicious activity, immediately change your password and contact our customer service at the number below.

How often should I change my password?

We recommend changing your password every 3 months for optimal security.
